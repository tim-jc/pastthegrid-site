Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Home Assistant Nest Integration

This application is used privately to connect Google Nest devices to a self-hosted Home Assistant installation. It is not offered as a public service.

Information accessed

The integration accesses Google Nest device information, including thermostat status, temperature, operating mode and settings, as permitted by the Google Smart Device Management API.

How information is used

Information is used solely to monitor and control Nest devices within the private Home Assistant installation, including through Apple Home.

Storage and sharing

Device information and authentication credentials are handled by the self-hosted Home Assistant installation. Information is not sold or used for advertising. Device information is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

Access and deletion

Access can be revoked through the relevant Google account. Locally stored integration data can be removed by deleting the Nest integration from Home Assistant.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact the application owner using the contact information supplied with the Google OAuth application.
